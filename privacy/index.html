Privacy policy

WireIt Job Sync · internal tool of WireIt CT LLC

This policy covers WireIt Job Sync, a private internal tool made by and for WireIt CT LLC. It connects only to WireIt CT LLC’s own QuickBooks Online company. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it reads and writes

  • Reads customers (names and addresses) and estimates (quotes: status, customer, job site address) from our QuickBooks company.
  • Creates a QuickBooks customer for a new job site, only when a WireIt staff member taps to approve it.
  • It does not read or change bank, payroll, payment or tax data.

Why

To keep our jobs in step between QuickBooks and our crew time-tracking app (Connecteam): when a quote is accepted in QuickBooks, it offers to create the matching job, with its name and address, in the time-tracking app, and it can create a matching QuickBooks customer for a job made there.

Where data is kept

Job names, addresses and the QuickBooks connection keys are stored only on WireIt CT LLC’s own office PC, readable only by its administrators. Job names and addresses are sent to our time-tracking app, and short job notifications go to WireIt staff phones through a push-notification service. The data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone else, and is not used for advertising.

Disconnecting

The connection can be removed at any time from QuickBooks (Settings → Apps / connected apps), or by deleting the saved keys on the office PC. After disconnecting, the tool reads nothing more from QuickBooks.
